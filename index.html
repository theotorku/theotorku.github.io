Operating Principles

How I Think

These aren't values statements. They're decision filters— the heuristics that shape how I evaluate tradeoffs and prioritize work.

01

Judgment over Credentials

What matters is the quality of decisions made under uncertainty, not the pedigree that preceded them.

02

Decisions over Timelines

The interesting question is never "when" but "why this, why now, why not something else."

03

Depth over Breadth

I'd rather understand one system completely than touch twelve superficially. Expertise compounds.

04

Clarity over Marketing

If I can't explain it simply, I don't understand it well enough. Jargon is a smell.

05

Signal over Noise

Everything that doesn't directly serve the goal is a distraction. Ruthless prioritization isn't mean—it's respectful.

06

Shipping over Polishing

A working product in front of one real user beats a perfect one in front of none. Built is not the same as adopted.
